  objective props, faction dressing, and map topology instead of 50 bespoke

* Missions 1-10 now have concrete environmental beats that can feed playable
  mission briefs and starter tileset planning.
* The five-mission arcs through Mission 50 give the full campaign a visual
  progression while keeping later content modular.

## Challenges And Risks

* Environment variety can become scope and readability debt if every mission
  becomes a bespoke tileset or gains a new terrain mechanic.
* Late-game Loom, refinery, fog-line, settlement, and coalition maps must remain
  visually distinct without overwhelming 64px tiles, unit labels, HP bars,
  cursor markers, movement highlights, attack highlights, and objective markers.
* Any special object that affects movement, combat, visibility, capture,
  production, supply, scoring, or AI priority still needs inspect UI,
  forecasts, deterministic tests, and replay data before implementation.
* The worktree contains unrelated in-progress changes from other passes, so
  future reports and commits should keep environment-planning attribution
  scoped.
