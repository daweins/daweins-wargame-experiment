Finish the ready foundation backlog for deterministic tactical systems while

## Time In Progress

The autonomous effort remains in the same-day first-prototype phase. This report

## How Work Is Being Done

* The pass stayed C#-first and kept gameplay truth in `Wargame.Core`.
* Each promoted story was implemented as a bounded deterministic fixture with
  smoke coverage before moving from Proposed to Completed Foundation.
* Tracking files were updated in `.copilot-tracking/agentic/` without secrets,
  private device details, ignored logs, or raw local-only output.

## Current State

* G010 Capture economy fixture is complete. The core now tracks controlled
  properties, player income, player funds, capture income changes, and income
  payout at the next player turn.
* G011 Light supply fixture is complete. Limited ammo is opt-in, empty limited
  ammo units cannot attack, forecasts show zero damage when empty, and Field
  Rigs can wait to restore one ammo to an adjacent friendly limited-ammo unit.
* G012 CO power scaffold is complete. `ActivatePower` now supports Rusk's
  `lock-the-line` command, charge gain from surviving damage, enemy-phase
  defense for held-position friendly ground units, spending four charge, and
  deterministic expiration on the next player turn.
* `CampaignAutoplayer` now describes `ActivatePower`, and replay DTO support in
  the smoke project covers the new command shape.
* G045 remains blocked until returned transparent unit atlas art is saved for
  review and promotion.

## What Has Worked Well

* Economy, ammo, and CO-power state are included in clone/hash paths, keeping
  replay and smoke fixtures honest.
* The new systems are opt-in or tightly scoped, so existing campaign balance is
  preserved until authored missions intentionally use the rules.
* The CO-power scaffold uses an explicit command instead of hidden automation,
  which keeps future replay, UI, and AI work straightforward.
