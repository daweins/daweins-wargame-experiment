## Prompt

Copy this into ChatGPT image generation:

```text
Create a 320x180 pixel-art cutscene frame for the opening mission of a grounded
near-future tactical game.

Scene: a temporary survey camp HQ in a dusty basin valley at low morning light.
There are prefab shelters, basalt ridges, a jammed relay mast, floodlights, and
a distant warning marker showing where Scout-7 went missing.

Composition: cinematic but simple, with readable large shapes and clear layers
that could later be rebuilt from rectangles, ellipses, polygons, and dithering.

Mood: practical field science becoming a battlefield, tense but not melodramatic.

Style: crisp 16-bit-era tactical pixel art for a grounded near-future sci-fi
wargame, readable silhouettes, limited palette, clean dark outlines, strong
terrain and unit readability at Steam Deck handheld distance, no photorealism,
no painterly texture, no excessive bloom, no tiny unreadable detail. The art
should feel compatible with an Advance Wars-like tactical board while looking
more grounded, practical, and militarized.
```
