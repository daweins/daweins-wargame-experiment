## Prompt

Copy this into ChatGPT image generation:

```text
Create a concept sheet of 64x64 terrain tiles for a 2D turn-based tactical
combat game.

Tiles to include: dusty basin plain, exposed service road, survey crate cover,
basalt ridge, temporary Kestrel prefab HQ.

Faction and setting: Kestrel research expedition forced to defend a survey camp
in a near-future resource conflict.

Requirements: each tile must be visually distinct at small size, use a shared
limited palette, include clear tactical readability, and avoid decorative noise
that would hide units or cursor overlays.

Style: crisp 16-bit-era tactical pixel art for a grounded near-future sci-fi
wargame, readable silhouettes, limited palette, clean dark outlines, strong
terrain and unit readability at Steam Deck handheld distance, no photorealism,
no painterly texture, no excessive bloom, no tiny unreadable detail. The art
should feel compatible with an Advance Wars-like tactical board while looking
more grounded, practical, and militarized.
```
