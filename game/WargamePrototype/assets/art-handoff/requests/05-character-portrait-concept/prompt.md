## Prompt

Copy this into ChatGPT image generation:

```text
Create a small character portrait concept for a grounded near-future tactical
game.

Character: a Kestrel field commander who started as a research expedition lead
and is now trying to make battlefield decisions with imperfect equipment. The
expression should be tired, sharp, dryly amused, and under pressure.

Framing: shoulders-up portrait suitable for a dialogue box. Include a practical
field jacket, headset or comms hardware, and restrained blue-gray Kestrel color
accents.

Style: 16-bit-era pixel art portrait, clean silhouette, limited palette, crisp
features, readable expression, no photorealism, no painterly texture, no excess
detail that would vanish in a small dialogue UI.
```
