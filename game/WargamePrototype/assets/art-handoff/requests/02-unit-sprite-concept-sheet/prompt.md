## Prompt

Copy this into ChatGPT image generation:

```text
Create a concept sheet of tactical unit sprites for a 2D turn-based wargame.

Units to include: infantry squad, armored line vehicle, fast scout buggy,
engineer vehicle, anti-armor lancer unit, field repair rig.

Faction treatment: player units use practical blue-gray Kestrel colors with
small cyan accents; enemy variants should support a later orange-red corporate
palette swap.

Requirements: each unit must have a distinct silhouette at 64x64 size, a clear
front or three-quarter tactical read, strong team-color accent placement, and no
fine details that disappear when scaled down.

Style: crisp 16-bit-era tactical pixel art for a grounded near-future sci-fi
wargame, readable silhouettes, limited palette, clean dark outlines, strong
terrain and unit readability at Steam Deck handheld distance, no photorealism,
no painterly texture, no excessive bloom, no tiny unreadable detail. The art
should feel compatible with an Advance Wars-like tactical board while looking
more grounded, practical, and militarized.
```
